# Credits

OntoDL+ was created by **Alexandre Costa Dias** at the University of Minho, Escola de Engenharia, Departamento de Informática, as a master's dissertation in Informatics Engineering (October 2021).

Supervisors:

- **Pedro Rangel Henriques** (Ph.D.)
- **Cristiana Esteves Araújo** (M.Sc.)

The language extends **OntoDL**.

**Thesis:** Dias, Alexandre Costa. *ONTODL+: an ontology description language and its compiler.* Master's thesis, University of Minho, Braga, Portugal, 2021. <https://hdl.handle.net/1822/81340>

**Official site:** <https://epl.di.uminho.pt/~gepl/GEPL_DS/OntoDL/>

José Carlos Ramalho and Salete Teixeira provided ontologies used in the thesis. Those files are not copied in this repository; they remain on the [official download page](https://epl.di.uminho.pt/~gepl/GEPL_DS/OntoDL/download.html).
